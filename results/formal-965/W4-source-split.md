## Limits

cached_nonpresent with readahead_hit=1 is a cache-hit subset, not a disjoint disk-read population. swapped_read_io and swapped_read_cached are successful retrieval events; they do not identify physical source per page. Readback disk requests and retrieval-events-per-request are aggregate measurements, and the repeated-read value is only a lower bound against the snapshot memcomp cohort. The readback window may include continued writeback; its bytes are reported separately and are not divided by the pressure-to-read volume.
